    How this is used

    Your postcode is sent to postcodes.io, and the address list comes from OpenStreetMap, so it can be incomplete. If you add an address, it is sent to Claude along with the document text. Nothing is saved.

    Where to find the documents

    On your council's planning website, search for the application reference or address. The documents are usually listed under a "Documents" tab.

    What happens to your files

    Files are opened in your browser to check they can be read. When you choose Analyse, the text from them is sent to Claude, through this site's small relay, to write the summary. Nothing is stored. The files themselves aren't uploaded or stored.

    How to read this page

    Every line below is taken from the application documents and names its source page. Nothing here is a judgement on whether the scheme is good or bad.

    Stated

    The documents say this in writing, or clearly say nothing about it.

    Reading

    Our interpretation of the drawings and plans. Check the source if it matters to you.

    Well covered. The documents assess this topic in detail.

    Partly covered. The topic is mentioned, or only partly assessed.

    Not covered. The documents say nothing about it. That does not mean there is no impact.

    The coverage boxes show how much the documents say about a topic. They do not measure harm or benefit.
